TYPES
Classify by architectural relationships, not image slope or direction.
A perspective-diagonal edge can still be an eave, rake, ridge, hip, or valley.

FREE EDGE: roof surface on one side only.
- eave: the lower free edge of a roof slope, typically the gutter line.
- rake: a free sloping side edge of a gable.

MEETING LINE: roof surface on both sides.
- ridge: the upper meeting line of two roof slopes.
- hip: a sloping convex meeting line between roof slopes.
- valley: a sloping concave meeting line between roof slopes.

- junction: a visible boundary where a roof surface meets a wall.
- unknown: a visible structural roof edge whose type is uncertain.
